How to play

Your runner sprints forward on its own. Survive as long as you can, read the hazards early and grab energy orbs.

  • Energy barrier (low, amber) – JUMP over it.
  • Laser gate (high beam, violet) – SLIDE under it.
  • Barricades & freight pods (tall, crossed or striped) – CHANGE LANE. They cannot be jumped.
  • Energy orbs – +10 points each. Arcs of orbs show the ideal jump.

Keyboard

←/A left · →/D right
↑/W/Space jump · ↓/S slide
Esc/P pause

Touch

Swipe left or right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide. On-screen buttons can be enabled in Settings.

Score: 1 point per metre (more at higher speed levels) + 10 per orb + 50 for a last-moment dodge. Slide while airborne to drop fast.

Settings

Saving is unavailable in this browser mode. Settings and records last until you close the game.
